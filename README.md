## 📌 Overview

Pet Healthcare App is a comprehensive pet health management application built with Flutter. The app provides pet owners with tools to track their pet's health, manage appointments, and access various pet care services.

## 🚀 Tech Stack

- **Flutter** (UI Framework)
- **Material Design 3**
- **Custom Animations**
- **Charts and Analytics**
- **Interactive Maps**

## 🔑 Key Features

### Health Management
- ✅ **Health Tracking**: Monitor your pet's health metrics
- ✅ **Health History**: View detailed health records and charts
- ✅ **Prediction System**: Get insights about potential health issues
- ✅ **QR Code System**: Quick access to pet's medical records

### Pet Care
- ✅ **Pet Profile Management**: Add and manage multiple pets
- ✅ **Document Management**: Store and organize pet documents
- ✅ **Expense Tracking**: Monitor pet-related expenses
- ✅ **Reminders**: Set up medication and appointment reminders

### Community & Support
- ✅ **Reviews & Comments**: Share experiences and get feedback
- ✅ **Pet-Friendly Places**: Find nearby pet-friendly locations
- ✅ **Help Desk**: Access support when needed
- ✅ **FAQs**: Get answers to common questions
- ✅ **Chatbot**: Quick assistance for common queries

### User Experience
- ✅ **Smooth Animations**: Engaging user interface
- ✅ **Calendar Integration**: Manage appointments efficiently
- ✅ **Profile Customization**: Personalize your experience
- ✅ **Document Management**: Easy access to important files
